# Salsa & Agena Account Deletion

Last updated: July 3, 2026

Customers can request deletion of their Salsa & Agena app account at any time.

## How To Request Account Deletion

To request account deletion, contact the restaurant using one of the following methods:

- Owner/contact: מייס עקל
- Phone: 046452919
- Mobile/WhatsApp: 0547475042 / 0547057464 / 0547057463

Please include the phone number used to sign in to the app so we can identify the correct account.

## What Will Be Deleted Or Anonymized

After verifying the request, Salsa & Agena will delete or anonymize:

- Customer profile information.
- Phone number or personal identifiers connected to the account.
- Push notification tokens connected to the account.

## Order Records

Some order records may be retained where required for legal, accounting, fraud-prevention, or operational obligations.

If order records are retained, Salsa & Agena will remove or anonymize personal identifiers where reasonably possible.

## Demo Mode

The app includes a reviewer/demo mode. Demo mode does not create a real customer account and does not require account deletion.

## Processing Time

Account deletion requests are handled after verification. The restaurant may contact you if more information is needed to identify the account.
